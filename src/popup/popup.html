How it works

Press Start on a LinkedIn job search results page. With "Click through the jobs for me" ticked, the extension opens each job itself, a few seconds apart, saves its full details and pages on; keep that LinkedIn tab open and visible. Untick it to click the jobs yourself instead (you can switch while it runs). Press Stop to end. Jobs already saved are updated, not duplicated.
